# 0002. Audience

Date: 2026-11-15 · Status: accepted

## Decision
The site is written for senior, staff and principal engineers, tech leads and engineering managers who feel behind on AI. It teaches what to know, which skills to build and how to use AI in daily work. It does not go deep into model internals.

## Consequence
Every lesson assumes engineering experience and no AI background. Students and founders are not the target reader.
